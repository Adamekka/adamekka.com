# Portfolio

- Keep this a minimal, dark, single-page personal portfolio with projects and contacts.
- Group projects by year and use bundled UbuntuMono Nerd Font throughout the site.
- Keep the portfolio's color palette consistent with the owner's blog.
- Preserve the owner's direct tone. Do not invent biography, projects, or contact details.
- The contacts repository is the source of truth. Sync contacts during builds, never in the visitor's browser. No scheduled sync or CI is required.
- Keep the published site static and free of unnecessary client-side JavaScript.
